Frequently Asked Consumer Questions

Quick answers to the most common consumer queries in India. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us.

How do I file a consumer complaint in India?

You can file a consumer complaint through multiple channels: 1) National Consumer Helpline at 1800-11-4000 (toll-free), 2) Online via the e-Daakhil portal (edaakhil.nic.in), 3) Approaching your local District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission. For claims up to ₹50 lakh, approach the District Commission; up to ₹2 crore, the State Commission; and above ₹2 crore, the National Commission (NCDRC). ConsumerAdvice.in provides free templates and step-by-step guidance.

What are the 6 consumer rights under Indian law?

The Consumer Protection Act 2019 guarantees 6 fundamental rights: 1) Right to Safety - protection against hazardous goods, 2) Right to be Informed - about quality, quantity, potency, purity, standard and price, 3) Right to Choose - access to a variety of goods at competitive prices, 4) Right to be Heard - consumer interests to be considered, 5) Right to Seek Redressal - against unfair trade practices, 6) Right to Consumer Education - to be informed about rights and remedies.

How long does it take to resolve a consumer complaint in India?

Under the Consumer Protection Act 2019, consumer commissions are required to adjudicate complaints within 3 to 5 months from the date of filing (where no laboratory testing is required). However, practical timelines vary. Mediation, introduced under the 2019 Act, can resolve disputes within 3 months. Complex cases at the NCDRC level may take 6-12 months. Using the e-Daakhil portal often speeds up the process.

Can I return a product bought online if I simply don't like it?

Under Indian e-commerce rules, for most products, you have a right to return within the specified return window (typically 7-30 days depending on the platform and product category). Some items like underwear, personalized goods, and perishables are non-returnable. If the seller refuses a valid return, you can escalate to the platform's grievance officer and then to the consumer commission. Always check the return policy before purchasing.

What compensation can I claim as an Indian consumer?

You can claim: 1) Refund of the amount paid, 2) Replacement of defective product, 3) Compensation for mental agony, harassment, and physical suffering (typically ₹5,000 to ₹1,00,000+ depending on severity), 4) Litigation costs, 5) Removal of deficiency in service, 6) Punitive damages in cases of gross negligence. The commission has wide discretion in awarding compensation.
