    How to use this Date Calculator

    This Date Calculator gives accurate answers in seconds, right in your browser. Whether you need the number of days between two dates, a deadline 90 days from today, or an exact age, follow these steps.

    1. Choose a mode. Days between dates, Add or subtract, or Age.
    2. Select dates. Use the date pickers. Today’s date is filled in using Indian Standard Time (IST).
    3. Click Calculate. You get the result in years, months and days, plus total days, weeks, working days and hours.
    4. Adjust if needed. Tick “include end date” to count both the first and last day.

    Your dates stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded or stored, so the Date Calculator is private as well as free.

    How the Date Calculator works

    Dates are converted to UTC calendar days, so daylight saving time or time zones never shift the answer. The calculator then follows these rules:

    Total days = (end date − start date) ÷ 86,400,000 ms (+1 if end date is included)
    Years, months, days = calendar difference, borrowing days from the previous month when needed
    Working days = days from Monday to Friday in the range
    Add months = move the month, then limit the day to the last day of that month (31 Jan + 1 month = 28/29 Feb)

    Leap years are handled by the browser’s calendar: a year is a leap year when it divides by 4, except century years that do not divide by 400. So 2024 and 2028 are leap years, and 2100 is not.

    Date Calculator FAQ

    What is a Date Calculator?

    A Date Calculator is an online tool that finds the exact difference between two dates, or works out a new date by adding or subtracting days, months or years.

    How do I calculate the number of days between two dates?

    Enter the start date and end date, then click Calculate days. You get years, months, days, total days, weeks and working days instantly.

    How do I add days to a date?

    Open the Add or subtract tab, pick the start date, choose Add, enter the days, months or years, and click Calculate date to see the new date and weekday.

    Does the Date Calculator include the end date?

    Not by default. Tick “Include end date in the count” to count both the first and the last day.

    Does the Date Calculator handle leap years?

    Yes. Leap years, including 29 February, and the different lengths of months are handled automatically.

    How are working days counted?

    Working days count Monday to Friday. Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays are not adjusted for state-wise holidays, so check your local calendar for exact business days.

    How do I calculate my age in years, months and days?

    Open the Age tab, enter your date of birth, and the calculator shows your exact age, total days lived and days left until your next birthday.

    Is this Date Calculator free to use in India?

    Yes. It is completely free, needs no registration, uses the DD/MM/YYYY format and Indian Standard Time, and works on any phone or computer.
